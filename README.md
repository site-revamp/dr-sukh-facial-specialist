# Dr Sukh Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Dr Sukh is the professional practice of Mr Sukhpreet Singh Dubb, a dual-qualified facial surgeon based in Chelsea, London. The practice specialises in facial plastic and aesthetic surgery, including procedures for the upper face, midface, lower face and neck.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the practice’s specialist facial surgery positioning, presenting procedures more clearly and creating a more direct path for prospective patients to request a consultation.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Corrected Surgeon Profile Navigation
- Replaced the incorrect `/profile/` destination with the verified surgeon profile page: `/about/`.
- Ensures visitors can access the correct information about the surgeon and their professional background.

### 2. Improved Patient Results and Review Presentation
- Identified the need to replace text placeholders in the patient results section with actual, consented before-and-after photographs.
- Highlighted the need to display verified patient reviews and link them to the correct review profiles, rather than relying on generalised review summaries.
- These sections require the appropriate original images, review content and links before publication.

### 3. Strengthened Consultation and Medical Content
- Identified the need for an embedded consultation form on the homepage instead of directing visitors to another page.
- Addressed the limitations of the existing contact form, including JavaScript-dependent notices.
- Flagged procedure descriptions for review by the surgeon to ensure medical accuracy and avoid language that could suggest guaranteed outcomes.

## Design Improvements

- Organised the homepage around the practice’s specialist facial surgery services and treatment areas.
- Improved the presentation of the surgeon’s professional positioning and the range of procedures offered.
- Identified patient results, verified reviews and consultation access as key parts of the homepage experience.
- Focused the overall layout on clearer information hierarchy and a more direct patient enquiry journey.

## Implementation Notes

Before publication, add the consented patient photographs, verified reviews and working review-profile links. Complete and test the homepage consultation form, and obtain the surgeon’s approval of all medical procedure descriptions. Verify any additional contact details before displaying them.
